What the picture can establish

The annotated type of a neuron, its available location, and its recorded connections. “Incoming” and “outgoing” are relative to the selected neuron; most internal neurons have both.

What it cannot establish

Which neuron represents a thought or makes a maze decision. This is a static anatomical view, not live activity. Our generic model treats L1 as spiking, while biological L1 chiefly uses graded voltage changes; its simulated firing is not validated L1 physiology.
